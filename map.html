📅 Activity calendar
Day-by-day fire activity for the box you draw on the map. It flags unusual days and critical periods (2+ busy days in a row). Draw a box first (max ~17°×17°).
"Burned" = estimate: 1 km pixel per fire cell per day (VIIRS 0.14 km², MODIS 1 km²), not a burned-scar measurement. Darker = more fire events. Red outline = unusual day, amber = elevated, hatched = no data. "Unusual" means far above the typical day of this same area inside the chosen period. Click a day or a period to see it on the map.
